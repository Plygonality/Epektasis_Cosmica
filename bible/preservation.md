# Preservation

Competing Earth reconstructions from one finite archive. Production mapping: [`production.md`](production.md) §3.

## Canon

**CANON.** The departure archive supports different interpretations of preservation.

**CANON.** Some communities prioritise conditions documented near launch. Some reconstruct earlier ecosystems. Some continue adaptation and evolution. Some build new ecosystems from Earth-derived organisms.

**CANON.** These are practices and institutions. They are not automatically four named factions.

**CANON.** Archives are finite and uncertain. Reconstruction is distinguishable from verified historical recovery.

**CANON.** Do not imply that life has been detected on Kepler-62e or Kepler-62f.

**CANON.** The abiogenesis premise starts founding stock from validated instructions. It does not certify that a later ecosystem matches Earth.

## Narrative use

**CANON.** Newly recovered information can reveal that a long-established ecosystem differs from an Earth reference. Correcting the discrepancy can conflict with living communities already dependent on the derived system.

**CANON.** That conflict is compatible with an operational cell. It does not add a Time-slice epoch ID.
